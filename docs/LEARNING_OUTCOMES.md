# Learning objectives behind each change — and how to evaluate them

**No improvement in learning outcomes is claimed.** Each row states the *intended* learning
objective and a way an instructor could gather evidence. Suggested designs: compare cohorts
(before/after 3.0, or randomly assigned sections), use the same post-quiz, and read the recorded
help/attempt data in the PDF.

| Change | Learning objective it supports | How it could be evaluated |
|---|---|---|
| Separate physical inventory, backlog and net position; backlog column | Distinguish stock from unmet orders; see that backlog carries forward and is cleared by later production | Quiz item: "Month 8 shows 5,000 backlog and 0 inventory — what is next month's beginning position?"; error rate on the *Beginning Backlog* / *Backorders* columns |
| Safety stock as an explicit planning target; B ≠ S allowed | Explain what safety stock protects against and why it can be consumed | Short-answer coding of reflections; quiz with B ≠ S scenario |
| Explicit terminal requirements | Recognise that horizon-end conditions affect plan cost | Do students state the December requirement unprompted in justifications? |
| Idle paid capacity shown; worker-equivalents wording | Understand that labor cost follows capacity, not output | Quiz: cost of whole-worker rounding; compare whole vs partial answers |
| On-time fulfillment, months with shortages, ending backlog (replace "service level") | Choose and interpret service measures precisely | Coding of how justifications cite service; quiz distinguishing on-time from eventual fulfillment |
| Explicit "Check my work" (feedback after an attempt) | Retrieval/attempt before feedback; productive struggle | Compare first-attempt accuracy and number of checks (recorded in the PDF) with live-feedback sections |
| Formula errors explained (division by zero, circular, missing reference); MAX/ROUNDUP work | Build and debug spreadsheet models | Count of formula errors per student over time; transfer task in Excel |
| Excel hand-off with identical addressing and the same grading | Transfer the method to the tool used in practice | Proportion of students who use Excel; accuracy of imported vs typed plans |
| Hybrid challenge with feasibility panel, assistance labelled, like-for-like comparison | Judge a plan against constraints, not just cost; understand when flexibility pays | Rubric on hybrid write-ups (names the binding constraint?); share of attempts feasible |
| Best feasible vs cheaper infeasible kept apart | Feasibility precedes cost optimisation | Rubric item; frequency of reporting an infeasible plan as "best" |
| Presets (seasonality, hire/layoff costs, limited flex, storage) | Predict how parameters change the best strategy | Prediction-vs-result accuracy on preset scenarios |
| Forecast-error evaluation of a committed plan | Reason about robustness and safety stock under uncertainty | Do justifications cite shortage probability / bad-year cost? Change in safety-stock choices |
| Perishability with age cohorts | See the limits of an inventory-age heuristic | Quiz: why did the level plan lose bottles? |
| Optimization benchmark (after submission) | Calibrate a hand-built plan against a proven bound | Reflection prompt on the gap; "feasible vs proven optimal" vocabulary check |
| PDF report with verbatim writing | Communicate and justify a recommendation | Rubric scoring of the justification (graded by the instructor, not by plan cost) |

Plan cost is **not** an academic grade; any course grade should come from the instructor's rubric
applied to the submitted PDF.
